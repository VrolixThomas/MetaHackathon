By default the focused item is held until you press Next. The optional loop is a design demonstration, not proposed automatic review navigation. System reduced motion wins; Next then switches immediately. No mic, audio, API, real work items or job control.

The mark expresses selective attention, not more output. The three surfaces are a visual shorthand for current and ongoing work, not a fixed number of agents or providers. Focus is the design theme; these are direction labels rather than a wordmark or monogram.
