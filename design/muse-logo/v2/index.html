01 / Pocket creature

It reads as a little creature before it reads as a logo. The ears, bean body and two feet survive the simplified 16px drawing; gaze and springy squish add character at larger sizes. The firefly offers the clearest stable-core motion. The octopus best expresses parallel work, with the greatest small-size simplification cost.

Eight states. Eight readable attitudes.

Fixed reference poses for all three directions, also used under reduced motion. Text carries the exact state; expression supports it. Success celebrates completion of the current operation, not review acceptance, publication or delivery.
