State precedence / simulated signals

Resolved foreground state: idle

Foreground speaking and work defer needs-input. Background job signals belong to their own marks. Error/offline refer to this mark’s session, not unrelated providers.

Error → Offline → Speaking → Working → Needs input → Success → Listening → Idle. Barge-in clears playback first, so active listening takes over immediately.

01 / Why Openfold

Two soft folds hold a diagonal pocket of air. It feels like listening before answering. The same two pieces open without losing the compact resting silhouette.

02 / Deliberate motion

A short contraction gives way to a calm opening. Amplitude changes space, not color. Working has one slow fold cycle; idle, waiting and offline stay still.

03 / Built to hand off

Editable SVG paths, original outlined lettering and a dependency-free controller. No external fonts, requests, providers, audio or microphone permissions.
