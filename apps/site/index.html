Good questions.

What is MuseFocus?+

MuseFocus is a voice-first workspace for guiding work and reviewing what comes back. It keeps independent coding jobs moving in the background and gives you a focused place to review changes, ask questions and make decisions.

Does work move on without my review?+

Background progress and your approval are separate steps. Ready work comes back for review. Publishing or sending something requires the relevant confirmation; readiness is not an automatic merge.

Where does background work run?+

The current app runs background jobs on your Mac in separate workspaces. Your Mac needs to stay awake and connected. Hosted, always-on execution is not part of this version.

Can I download it yet?+

MuseFocus is in development. This page offers an interactive illustration of the workflow. A public download will be linked here when the release is ready.
